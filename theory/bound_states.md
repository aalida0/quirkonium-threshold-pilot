# Bound-state methods
src/spectrum.py uses a three-point O(h²) tridiagonal radial Schrödinger Hamiltonian; src/shooting.py uses an independent outward Numerov integration with Coulomb-cusp Frobenius initial data and a Brent root solve. Both operate on the same continuous Cornell model and separately converge. Selected comparison: Table III mQ100, sigma=.18, a=(4/3)*.091, Δ21=.377153 GeV compared with published .375; Tables V and VI show discrepancies at higher mQ, documented in validation/SPECTRUM_VALIDATION.md. Original grids: rmax=4 GeV^-1 for sigma=.18 and 1.5 GeV^-1 for sigma=18, step approx .0012 for mQ100 or .0006 for mQ500.
